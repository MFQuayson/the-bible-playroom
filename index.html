A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS.

Curious?
So are we.

Can we use this at home and at church?+

Yes. The starter pack includes a teaching guide and a shorter family session. Choose your children’s age group and use the activities that fit your time and setting.

How do the age groups work?+

Ages 3 to 5 explore with pictures, movement and adult support. Ages 6 to 8 connect ideas through choices and simple reading. Ages 9 to 12 investigate the passage, explain their thinking and apply it to everyday situations.

Which Bible translation do you use?+

Our teaching is prepared for use with the NIV or Good News Bible. Open your own Bible to the passage shown. Activity summaries are original teaching paraphrases, not quotations from a Bible translation.

Where can we watch the episodes?+

Episode 1, Choosing the Right Path, is available in Watch & Learn with matching Psalm 1:1–2 lessons for all three ages. Tap Watch to load the embedded player, or open the episode on our official YouTube channel. New lesson packs will appear after they have been reviewed and approved.

Do children need an account?+

No account is needed for these activities. Saved favourites and completed activities stay in this browser on this device. They do not sync between devices. A grownup can clear them in the playroom settings.
